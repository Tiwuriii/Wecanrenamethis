          Who lives next to San José's data centers?

          This map combines state health and pollution data, satellite heat readings and the city's own permit list. It shows the neighborhoods around every proposed and approved data center.

          1. Choose what the map showsUse the buttons at the top left to color neighborhoods by overall burden, air pollution, health, heat, income and language, or distance to a data center. Darker means more burdened.
          2. Click a project siteSee who lives within 1,000 feet, half a mile and a mile, including children, schools and clinics, and what pollution they already breathe.
          3. Compare districts and other statesThe Districts tab sums up each council district. Beyond San José shows California power prices and what other cities and states have done.
